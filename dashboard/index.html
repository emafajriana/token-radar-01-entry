How implied value is computed

implied = max(1, round(TVL × category multiple × growth multiplier × maturity multiplier))

growth multiplier = 1 + (clamped 7-day change ÷ 100) × 0.5, with the change clamped to −60%…+120%. Maturity multiplier = 0.6 + (min(age in days, 1095) ÷ 1095) × 0.4, using 365 days when the listing date is unknown. The category multiple is the median market-cap-per-TVL of the live-token protocols in the same category; when a category has fewer than 3 live protocols the global median across all live protocols is used instead. The multiple is clamped to 0.05×–1.25×.
